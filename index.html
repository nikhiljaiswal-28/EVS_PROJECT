How Air Pollution Affects Health

Your Lungs

Fine dust particles can penetrate deep into lungs, causing breathing problems and long-term damage.

Your Heart

Long-term exposure increases the risk of heart disease, heart attacks, and strokes.

Children & Seniors

Kids and elderly need extra protection - their bodies are more vulnerable to pollution.

Mental Health

Air pollution can affect mood, concentration, and cognitive function over time.
